# Prototype architecture

## 1. Data layer
Inputs can come from:
- financial/accounting systems
- HR and payroll
- energy/water management
- waste records
- governance and policy documents

## 2. AI/NLP layer
Illustrative services:
- document/entity extraction
- semantic classification of ESG evidence
- duplicate and inconsistency detection
- anomaly detection
- confidence scoring
- assisted narrative generation

## 3. ESG calculation layer
Transforms raw inputs into normalized indicators such as:
- energy consumption
- GHG intensity
- waste recycling rate
- permanent-contract share
- gender pay gap
- employee turnover

## 4. VSME reporting layer
Maps indicators to disclosure topics, records evidence, shows missing data, and produces a report-ready preview.

## 5. Audit/control layer
Every generated value should ideally retain:
- source document
- source date
- transformation/calculation
- validation status
- confidence score
- reviewer approval

## Research evaluation
A future empirical study can compare:
1. manual reporting time vs AI-assisted reporting time
2. number of detected inconsistencies
3. calculation accuracy
4. completeness of disclosure mapping
5. reviewer acceptance of generated narratives
6. usability for VSMEs
